Questions

What is Laplace?

+

Laplace is a physics-based digital twin for solar PV plants. It models every asset from the panels to the grid connection, checks each inverter and string against what physics expects for the current sunlight and temperature, and ranks every loss by what it costs.

How does Laplace find lost energy?

+

Every five minutes it compares each inverter's measured output with the output the physics model expects, reconciled to the export meter. Each loss is located to the inverter, combiner or string and priced, so crews fix the most valuable problem first.

What data does Laplace need?

+

A month of inverter and meter data is enough to start. A CSV export works, and so does a read-only OPC UA or MQTT connection.

Does Laplace control my plant?

+

No. Laplace connects read-only over OPC UA or MQTT, without control access, and is built to central cyber-security rules.

What does Laplace do beyond monitoring?

+

It runs day-ahead and intraday schedules in 15-minute blocks and calculates deviation penalties before they land. It also prepares PPA invoices, reconciles revenue meters, and sends work orders to the exact string, with sync to SAP PM or Maximo.

Who is Laplace for?

+

Solar developers and independent power producers, utilities and governments, developers and planners testing new projects, and data centres that need round-the-clock supply.
